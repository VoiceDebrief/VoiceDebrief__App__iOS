Ideas and where they stand

Every idea and request for the two apps, from the issues, the articles and the reality files (VoiceDebrief, SGit Vaults), with its status. This page and the Ideas screen in the app are generated from those files by website/admin/ideas.mjs; nothing here is written by hand. The rules that decide a status are at the top of that script, and every idea in the app shows the sentence that decided its own. The machine-readable form is ideas.json.
