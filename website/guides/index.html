Guides to every feature

A page per feature, with pictures of the screens and numbered arrows. The same guides are in the app, under Guide. What a feature is and whether it has been seen working is on Ideas and where they stand; what exists is the reality file.

GuideWhat it covers
Record a voice noteOne button records.
Open a note: listen, send, saveA note opens with its one sentence as the heading, then what has happened to it, the recording, the transcript and the debrief.
The library: every note, and what each one is doingEvery note kept on this device, newest first, by day.
Vaults: your notes, encrypted, on the device firstA vault is an encrypted, version-controlled folder you own.
Send a note to somebodyA recipient is a person, another device or an agent session that has given you their link.
What happened since you last lookedWhen you open the app, a card on the Record tab says what is new: an update, with a link to what changed, and what happened to your notes since you last opened it.
Settings: language, saving, identity, creditThe few things to set once, and the places for the rest: who you are, who you send to, and the experiments.

Pictures: iPhone, light, from screens.yml run 39 on dev (commit 1d21781, 14 November 2026). Newer builds may differ: the record screen now chooses who a note is for above the record control.

VoiceDebrief iOS

The VoiceDebrief transcript and debrief as a native iPhone and iPad app, on Apple's own models, with the results in a vault you own. In development: there is nothing to install yet. Content CC BY 4.0; code Apache 2.0.
