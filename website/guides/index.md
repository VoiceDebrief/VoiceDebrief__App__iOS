# Guides to every feature

A page per feature, with pictures of the screens and numbered arrows. The same guides are in the app, under Guide. What a feature is and whether it has been seen working is on [Ideas and where they stand](../ideas/index.md); what exists is the [reality file](../built/index.md).

| Guide | What it covers |
|---|---|
| [Record a voice note](record/index.md) | One button records. |
| [Open a note: listen, send, save](note/index.md) | A note opens with its one sentence as the heading, then what has happened to it, the recording, the transcript and the debrief. |
| [The library: every note, and what each one is doing](library/index.md) | Every note kept on this device, newest first, by day. |
| [Vaults: your notes, encrypted, on the device first](vaults/index.md) | A vault is an encrypted, version-controlled folder you own. |
| [Send a note to somebody](recipients/index.md) | A recipient is a person, another device or an agent session that has given you their link. |
| [What happened since you last looked](briefing/index.md) | When you open the app, a card on the Record tab says what is new: an update, with a link to what changed, and what happened to your notes since you last opened it. |
| [Settings: language, saving, identity, credit](settings/index.md) | The few things to set once, and the places for the rest: who you are, who you send to, and the experiments. |

*Pictures: iPhone, light, from screens.yml run 39 on dev (commit 1d21781, 14 November 2026). Newer builds may differ: the record screen now chooses who a note is for above the record control.*
