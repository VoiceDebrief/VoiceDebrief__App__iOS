# What changed in v0.1.104

Released 14 November 2026. Every push to the repository's `dev` branch is a release; this page was written by CI from the notes that came with it. [Every version](../index.md) · [What is built](../../built/index.md)

The voice game shows what it hears, at the owner's request ("the game is so cool"): while Listen is on, a line under the board shows the sentence being said, black once settled and grey while it may still change, and the last four sentences with what each became (the command and which engine, refused, not understood, or asking the model) and its time; the command also sits beside the large time. The board takes the house palette, and Play as me puts your own avatar on the board in Boxes. Play opens on a landing page: the two games as large tiles with a picture of their first level, your best score and where you are, how to start, and how the last game went; a tile opens that game.

Pictures of the screens are added to this page when the screens workflow has taken them for this version.
