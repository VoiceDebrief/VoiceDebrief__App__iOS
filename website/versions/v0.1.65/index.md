# What changed in v0.1.65

Released 14 November 2026. Every push to the repository's `dev` branch is a release; this page was written by CI from the notes that came with it. [Every version](../index.md) · [What is built](../../built/index.md)

From the owner's drive, where signal came and went: a send that fails is tried five times (10 s, 30 s, 1 min, 2 min apart) and at once when iOS says the network is back, with "No connection, trying again in ..." and Try now on the card; after five tries, Send again, and it goes once more when the signal returns or the app opens. A sent note now ends in a large green "Sent to ... at ..." with a success tap, held for ten seconds.

Pictures of the screens are added to this page when the screens workflow has taken them for this version.
