# What changed in v0.1.97

Released 14 November 2026. Every push to the repository's `dev` branch is a release; this page was written by CI from the notes that came with it. [Every version](../index.md) · [What is built](../../built/index.md)

The Lab's control centre shows more of what it is doing, after the owner tried it ("really cool") and asked to see everything being heard and to follow the flow. While it listens, a strip at the top shows the words as they arrive, the ones that may still change in grey. Below, every sentence heard has its own numbered card, newest first, whether or not it led to an action: what was heard, what the fixed phrases made of it and which phrase it was, what the model made of it and how long it took, what was proposed, and what happened.

Pictures of the screens are added to this page when the screens workflow has taken them for this version.
