# What changed in v0.1.119

Released 15 November 2026. Every push to the repository's `dev` branch is a release; this page was written by CI from the notes that came with it. [Every version](../index.md) · [What is built](../../built/index.md)

A Lab experiment, L07, edit with Apple Intelligence, started small as the owner asked. Give it a text (typed, pasted, or a note's words as a copy) and a request in your own words: make it shorter, more formal, as bullet points, fix the names. Apple's model proposes the edited text, and the app shows the change itself, word by word: what goes is struck through and what comes in is underlined, with the before and the after a tap away. Accept or reject; an accepted change can be put back. It says so when nothing changed or when the result is suspiciously long or short. The note the words came from is never changed. In test: no edit has been asked of a model. Also written up, with no code: what it would take to play YouTube videos in the app, to connect Spotify or Apple Music, and which other accounts are worth connecting (issue 051).
