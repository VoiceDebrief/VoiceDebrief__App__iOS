# What changed in v0.1.147

Released 17 November 2026. Every push to the repository's `dev` branch is a release; this page was written by CI from the notes that came with it. [Every version](../index.md) · [What is built](../../built/index.md)

SGit CRM: your Gmail inside the CRM. Sign in with Google (Inbox), read your mail, see who is already in the CRM, add a sender with one tap, and reply or write; an email goes only when you press Send and confirm, and one sent to someone in the CRM is kept as an interaction with them. Needs an iOS OAuth client made in Google Cloud for the app, whose id you paste or scan in once. Works beside the mailbox twin, not instead of it.

Pictures of the screens are added to this page when the screens workflow has taken them for this version.
