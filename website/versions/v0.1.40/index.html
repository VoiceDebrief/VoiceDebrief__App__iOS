What changed in v0.1.40

Released 14 November 2026. Every push to the repository's dev branch is a release; this page was written by CI from the notes that came with it. Every version · What is built

Review round 3: five synthetic users on the pictures of finish and send, the briefing on opening and where the work happens. Three of round 2's findings are seen to be fixed; fifteen new ones, the first that a send cannot be stopped where it is pressed and is lost if the app is closed before it goes (issues 043 to 045). Nothing in the app has changed.

After review round 3: a send that is waiting is kept on the note, so closing the app does not forget it, and goes when the app next opens; a failed send is kept and said on the row, the note and the briefing; the card after Finish and send has Do not send; a note already sent to someone asks before sending again (issue 043). The balance and the version show as words in the bar, and Discard moves to the right while recording (044). Settings and the status screen say what is true: an interrupted recording is always kept, and vault keys are in iCloud Keychain by default (045). Rows say when a note was sent; the briefing keeps to three lines.

Pictures of the screens are added to this page when the screens workflow has taken them for this version.
