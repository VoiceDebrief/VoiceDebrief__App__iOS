# What changed in v0.1.68

Released 14 November 2026. Every push to the repository's `dev` branch is a release; this page was written by CI from the notes that came with it. [Every version](../index.md) · [What is built](../../built/index.md)

A note made for someone (a contact or Claude) goes as soon as its transcript is ready, without a debrief: the owner found the wait broke his flow, and the recipient reads the transcript (on his 13:31 note the debrief took 10.4 s after a 7 s transcript). A note for "Just me" is debriefed as before.

Pictures of the screens are added to this page when the screens workflow has taken them for this version.
