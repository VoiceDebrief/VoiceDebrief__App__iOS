# What changed in v0.1.62

Released 14 November 2026. Every push to the repository's `dev` branch is a release; this page was written by CI from the notes that came with it. [Every version](../index.md) · [What is built](../../built/index.md)

The sgit app becomes a secrets manager, first part. Secrets on the home list: passwords, device passcodes, Wi-Fi, cards, tokens and keys, each kept as one encrypted file in a vault, hidden values behind Face ID, a strong-password maker. Questions for you: the people building the app ask, and you answer by choosing, typing or speaking. The design, with drawings, is on the site. Your iPhone, iPad and Mac now open the same vaults with nothing to set up: vault keys and the access key are kept in iCloud Keychain (a switch in Settings), and vaults from your other devices appear when the app comes to the front. Sharing with people comes next

The sgit app: an experiment with Apple Intelligence. On a vault's three dots, Ask this vault puts one question to Apple's on-device model, which may look at the names, sizes, folders and history through four small tools, each call shown under the answer. It sees no file's contents, changes nothing, and nothing leaves the device. Untried on a device
