# What changed in v0.1.53

Released 14 November 2026. Every push to the repository's `dev` branch is a release; this page was written by CI from the notes that came with it. [Every version](../index.md) · [What is built](../../built/index.md)

After the owner's test on a Mac: the record control is at the top of the record screen and larger (pinned above the live words while recording); the contact chips are centred under the title instead of escaping to the edge; and the card after a note finished for someone shows its journey step by step (recorded, written up, encrypted and sent, part by part) with what goes, clearing itself once sent. No OK to press; Do not send only until sending starts.

Pictures of the screens are added to this page when the screens workflow has taken them for this version.
