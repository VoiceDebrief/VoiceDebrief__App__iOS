# What changed in v0.1.133

Released 16 November 2026. Every push to the repository's `dev` branch is a release; this page was written by CI from the notes that came with it. [Every version](../index.md) · [What is built](../../built/index.md)

SGit Secrets, second building block: when two devices changed a vault while apart, their changes are now joined. Different secrets merge on their own; a secret changed on both keeps the newer change and the older one beside it, so nothing is lost; a change is never undone by a deletion on the other device. Nothing on screen yet.
