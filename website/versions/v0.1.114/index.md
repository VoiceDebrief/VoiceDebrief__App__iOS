# What changed in v0.1.114

Released 15 November 2026. Every push to the repository's `dev` branch is a release; this page was written by CI from the notes that came with it. [Every version](../index.md) · [What is built](../../built/index.md)

A voice button on text fields, at the owner's request ("a little voice button everywhere we have an input"): tap the microphone beside a field and speak; the words appear live and stay editable. On the Lab, Your words, vault and contact names, the key name, an agent's message box and the picture sheet's line. Not on the lines added while recording, where the note itself has the microphone.

A voice button on text fields, at the owner's request: a small microphone beside a field fills it by speech on this device, the words appearing as they are said after anything already typed; tap again to stop, and the text stays editable. One listener for every field, never while a note is being recorded, and it stops when the app leaves the foreground. On the Lab's prompt and instructions, Your words, a vault's name, a contact's name and the key's name; not on keys, tokens or links.

Pictures of the screens are added to this page when the screens workflow has taken them for this version.
