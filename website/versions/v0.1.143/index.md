# What changed in v0.1.143

Released 16 November 2026. Every push to the repository's `dev` branch is a release; this page was written by CI from the notes that came with it. [Every version](../index.md) · [What is built](../../built/index.md)

SGit CRM: what the agents are doing. Choose the vault your conductor writes its report into, and Agents shows who is working, who is waiting or stuck and on what, what waits on you, who talks to whom, and the latest messages, read again every 30 seconds. Read only for now.

SGit CRM: cards and keys. Make your keys, show your contact card as a QR code, scan someone else's at a stand, check it is really them, and add their key to their record. A card holds only public keys; a changed one is refused.

SGit CRM: try it with sample data. One switch fills a separate sample vault with invented people from a Web Summit, their organisations, follow-ups in every state, two introductions and a draft, so the app can be shown before real contacts are in it; your own records are not touched. Import can also be tried on invented sample files.

Pictures of the screens are added to this page when the screens workflow has taken them for this version.
