# What changed in v0.1.102

Released 14 November 2026. Every push to the repository's `dev` branch is a release; this page was written by CI from the notes that came with it. [Every version](../index.md) · [What is built](../../built/index.md)

The record screen on iPad and Mac is two columns, at the owner's request: on the left who it is for, the control, and while recording the live words and attachments; on the right your recent notes, Messages from Claude (from your agents vault) and Explore. iPhone keeps one column. Play has its own drawing.

A wide record screen for iPad and Mac, ready to be wired: two columns that scroll on their own, what you do on the left (who it is for, the control, the live words) and what came back on the right (recent notes, Claude's replies as a conversation), held to readable widths.

Two ways to talk says where a shared vault is used now: in VoiceDebrief, the Agents vault you share with Claude; in SGit Vaults, a vault shared with the people you choose (it had said shared vaults with people were still to come). The Play section has its own drawing: a board of squares beside a figure carrying a lantern up a ladder.

Pictures of the screens are added to this page when the screens workflow has taken them for this version.
