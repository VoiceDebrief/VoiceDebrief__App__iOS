# What changed in v0.1.72

Released 14 November 2026. Every push to the repository's `dev` branch is a release; this page was written by CI from the notes that came with it. [Every version](../index.md) · [What is built](../../built/index.md)

The sgit app gains People (not yet on its root list): your card as a QR code and a link, contacts added by scanning, a picture or a link, with a photo of the person offered after scanning in person, an inbox that checks for new messages, and Keys and access for the keys your identity holds. Keys are made only when you ask.

Pictures of the screens are added to this page when the screens workflow has taken them for this version.
