# What changed in v0.1.57

Released 14 November 2026. Every push to the repository's `dev` branch is a release; this page was written by CI from the notes that came with it. [Every version](../index.md) · [What is built](../../built/index.md)

Agents, a new section made for iPad: how many agents are working for you, waiting or idle; each with its role, what it is doing and what it waits for; and a conversation with each. It is drawn from a report the conductor (Claude) writes into a vault of yours: you choose the vault and give it to Claude, its key sealed on the device to Claude's key. Messages to an agent go over its lane, or through Claude when it has none; replies come back in the report. The app refreshes every two minutes while you are there.

Pictures of the screens are added to this page when the screens workflow has taken them for this version.
