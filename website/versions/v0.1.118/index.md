# What changed in v0.1.118

Released 15 November 2026. Every push to the repository's `dev` branch is a release; this page was written by CI from the notes that came with it. [Every version](../index.md) · [What is built](../../built/index.md)

The voice games take their place in the app's one microphone turn: Listen and the voice player hold it while they listen or speak and give it back however they stop, so a voice field, the Lab and a game never listen at once; if something else holds it, the game says so. The game's text field has the shared voice button.
