What changed in v0.1.70

Released 14 November 2026. Every push to the repository's dev branch is a release; this page was written by CI from the notes that came with it. Every version · What is built

The articles can be read inside the app, under Guide, Articles (and SGit Vaults has its own copy to show, with its design pages); a design page can list its ideas for the ideas tracker under a heading Ideas. Release cards: an ASCII box per change, written to website/versions/next/, moved onto the version's page at release with the version and date filled in, and shown at the top of What is new; this release carries the first. A fourth guide for SGit Vaults: secrets, one secret, and the questions for the owner.

Pictures of the screens are added to this page when the screens workflow has taken them for this version.
