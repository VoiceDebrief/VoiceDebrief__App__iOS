# What changed in v0.1.142

Released 16 November 2026. Every push to the repository's `dev` branch is a release; this page was written by CI from the notes that came with it. [Every version](../index.md) · [What is built](../../built/index.md)

SGit CRM, at the owner's request: vault apps. Add a vault by its key or a QR code and open its own app inside the CRM, so a feature can be tried and changed without an app build; share a vault as a QR code (read only unless you choose the full key); and choose for each section whether it runs in the app or from a vault app. [Explorers and villagers](../../crm/explorers/index.md). Two more workflows mapped: what the agents are doing, and exchanging keys by QR.

SGit CRM, fourth step: who needs what. Follow-ups shows everyone to follow up by what is overdue, due today, this week, later, waiting on someone else, or done, with their organisation, owner and stage, and a filter by owner. Tap one to change its status, next step or date; the change is kept in your vault, signed. Introductions lists who should meet whom, why, and whether it happened.

SGit CRM, fifth step: drafts, never sends. From a follow-up, draft an email or a message; read and change it; send it yourself from the phone's own Mail or Messages, already filled in. Only when you press Send there is it marked sent, and the message is kept as an interaction with that person. The app never sends anything itself.

Pictures of the screens are added to this page when the screens workflow has taken them for this version.
