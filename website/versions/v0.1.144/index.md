# What changed in v0.1.144

Released 16 November 2026. Every push to the repository's `dev` branch is a release; this page was written by CI from the notes that came with it. [Every version](../index.md) · [What is built](../../built/index.md)

SGit CRM demo vaults: one shared set of invented data on the vault server (a mailbox twin, team sources, and a vault that lists them), added in the app by scanning one QR code. [How to add it](../../crm/demo/index.md). Keys travel only as QR codes, copied text or screenshots, never in a repository.

SGit CRM reads your mailbox's twin: open the vault the email cell fills, and everyone you correspond with is proposed, each email thread kept as an interaction, and a follow-up where someone wrote last and is waiting for your reply. Only verified senders count. Try it now with an invented mailbox (Import, Try with a sample mailbox); your real one is connected on your own device.

SGit CRM is in TestFlight: builds 0.1.142 (19) and 0.1.143 (20) were uploaded on the evening of 16 November, and the owner confirmed the app opens on a device.

Pictures of the screens are added to this page when the screens workflow has taken them for this version.
