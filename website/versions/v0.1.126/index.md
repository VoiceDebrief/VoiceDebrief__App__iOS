# What changed in v0.1.126

Released 15 November 2026. Every push to the repository's `dev` branch is a release; this page was written by CI from the notes that came with it. [Every version](../index.md) · [What is built](../../built/index.md)

The right of the record screen on an iPad or a Mac is now tabs, at the owner's request: Live (memos to Claude and their answers), Updates (what Claude sends without being asked, by stream: progress, builds, the newsroom, with pictures), Agents (everyone working for you, grouped by project, with a count of who is working or waiting and what each is doing) and Notes (your recent notes and the rest of the app). Each tab shows a red count of what is new since you last opened it, and the app remembers the tab. The line between the two columns can be dragged to give either side more room, and the split is remembered; a double tap puts it back. All of it comes from the agents vault you gave Claude: nobody else using the app sees it.

Pictures of the screens are added to this page when the screens workflow has taken them for this version.
