# What changed in v0.1.48

Released 14 November 2026. Every push to the repository's `dev` branch is a release; this page was written by CI from the notes that came with it. [Every version](../index.md) · [What is built](../../built/index.md)

Every part of the app is in plain sight, at the owner's request: the sidebar (tabs and More on iPhone) now lists Record, Library, Contacts, Vaults, and under You: Identity, Credit, Your activity; under Experiments: Lab, Your words; under More: What this build does, Settings. The record screen ends with Explore: a tile for every section. Your activity: every screen opened and for how long, and the main actions, kept on the device with no content, and a button to send it to Claude (developer).

Pictures of the screens are added to this page when the screens workflow has taken them for this version.
