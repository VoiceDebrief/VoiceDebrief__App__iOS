# What changed in v0.1.82

Released 14 November 2026. Every push to the repository's `dev` branch is a release; this page was written by CI from the notes that came with it. [Every version](../index.md) · [What is built](../../built/index.md)

The sgit app draws who you trust: you in the middle, each person around you with their photo, the line solid when you both have each other's card and dashed when only you have theirs. From the map, make a shared vault for chosen people: the vault's key is sealed to each person's key and sent to their inbox, and the vault shows who it is shared with

The sgit app gains People: your card as a QR code, contacts you add by scanning (with a photo of the person, kept on your device), an inbox for sealed messages, and Keys and access under Secrets

The sgit app keeps a small picture and the facts of every file in the vault beside it, so a folder of large videos shows its pictures without anyone downloading the videos: the picture (a photo, a frame of a video, a page of a document) and a facts file (size, dimensions, length, codec, camera, when it was taken; where only if you allow it) in a hidden .sgit folder that any client can read

Pictures of the screens are added to this page when the screens workflow has taken them for this version.
