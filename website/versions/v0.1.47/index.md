# What changed in v0.1.47

Released 14 November 2026. Every push to the repository's `dev` branch is a release; this page was written by CI from the notes that came with it. [Every version](../index.md) · [What is built](../../built/index.md)

The record screen is contact-driven, at the owner's request: above the record control, choose who the note is for (Just me, Claude (developer), or anyone you have added; the choice is remembered). Recording for someone means finishing is the send: tap to record, tap to finish, and it goes when it is written up, with Do not send on the card that follows. The built-in recipient is now called Claude (developer). The Lab is under Settings, Experiments.

Pictures of the screens are added to this page when the screens workflow has taken them for this version.
