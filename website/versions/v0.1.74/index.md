# What changed in v0.1.74

Released 14 November 2026. Every push to the repository's `dev` branch is a release; this page was written by CI from the notes that came with it. [Every version](../index.md) · [What is built](../../built/index.md)

The sgit app plays videos: a play mark on each video, a Play button that decrypts and plays it with sound (even on silent), full screen, and a picture from the video on its tile once it has been played

The sgit app has a Guide on its home list: what is new in each version, a guide with numbered pictures for each part (getting started, inside a vault, sharing, secrets), and every idea with where it stands

Pictures of the screens are added to this page when the screens workflow has taken them for this version.
