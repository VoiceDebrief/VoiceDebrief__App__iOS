# What changed in v0.1.55

Released 14 November 2026. Every push to the repository's `dev` branch is a release; this page was written by CI from the notes that came with it. [Every version](../index.md) · [What is built](../../built/index.md)

A Guide, for finding out what exists: every idea and request for both apps, from the issues, the articles and the reality files, with its status (seen working, built untried, in progress, proposed, parked), where it is in the app, the versions that named it and the sentence that decided its status, searchable in the app and on the site's new [ideas](../../ideas/index.md) page; What is new, every version newest first with those since you last looked marked; and a guide per feature (record, a note, the library, vaults, sending, the briefing, settings), each a picture of the screen with numbered arrows, in the app and as the site's new [guides](../../guides/index.md). The Guide and the welcome appear once they are given their place in the app's shell. Generated by `website/admin/ideas.mjs`, never written by hand.
