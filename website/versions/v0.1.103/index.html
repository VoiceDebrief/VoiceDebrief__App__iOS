What changed in v0.1.103

Released 14 November 2026. Every push to the repository's dev branch is a release; this page was written by CI from the notes that came with it. Every version · What is built

The Guide, after the owner found it had little to do on a wide Mac window: it opens on the latest release card, large, with Try buttons that open the sections the card names; on iPad and Mac it has two columns, the list on the left and the chosen card, guide or list on the right; every guide is a picture tile, and a guide shows each picture beside its numbered notes with an Open button for its section; the ideas are a count per status and what is in progress now. After the owner's second note: back and forward in the Guide as in a browser (two buttons, a sideways swipe, Command-[ and Command-]), a few lines before every picture saying what it shows and why it matters, a contents row at the top of each guide to jump to its parts, and wider pages.

Pictures of the screens are added to this page when the screens workflow has taken them for this version.
