# What changed in v0.1.117

Released 15 November 2026. Every push to the repository's `dev` branch is a release; this page was written by CI from the notes that came with it. [Every version](../index.md) · [What is built](../../built/index.md)

A voice button on text fields, at the owner's request: a small microphone beside a field fills it by speech on this device, the words appearing as they are said after anything already typed; tap again to stop, and the text stays editable. One microphone for the whole app: a field, the Lab's control centre and its live labels take turns, and whichever asks second is told something else is listening or speaking; never while a note is being recorded, and it stops when the app leaves the foreground. On the Lab's prompt and instructions, Your words, a vault's name, a contact's name and the key's name; not on keys, tokens or links.

Pictures of the screens are added to this page when the screens workflow has taken them for this version.
