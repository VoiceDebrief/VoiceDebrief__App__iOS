Articles

Ideas, positions and briefs for this project, written down so that later work takes them into account. They are intention and opinion, not fact: what is built is the only page that says what exists.

Most of these come from the owner's voice memos, written up by the agent working on the repository. Each says where it came from.

ArticleKindIn one line
The labBriefUse this app to find out what Apple's platform can do, before deciding what the product is
Two decision enginesPositionAn app now has two things that decide: the code, and a model. Keep the model on a short lead, one prompt at a time
A harness inside the appBriefGive the model its network, files, keys and vaults through separate doors, and draw what passes through them
The vault is a version-controlled storePosition and briefA vault needs no server to exist. A server is a remote you add
Where each key livesPosition and briefVault keys and your own key travel through iCloud Keychain; a device's key never leaves it; you vouch for your devices; optionally the register vouches for you
Voice everywhereIdeaVoice as the way in from every screen, and a recording that is never lost
Synthetic usersBriefInvented users walking the screens, to find where a real one would get lost
VoiceDebrief iOS

The VoiceDebrief transcript and debrief as a native iPhone and iPad app, on Apple's own models, with the results in a vault you own. In development: there is nothing to install yet. Content CC BY 4.0; code Apache 2.0.
