VoiceDebrief iOS / identity / return

Back from pki.sgit.ai

You asked pki.sgit.ai to vouch for the VoiceDebrief app on one of your devices, and it sent you here with its answer. The answer is in this page's address, after the #. It never left your browser: that part of an address is not sent to any server, and this page has no script that could read it.

Hand the answer to the app

  1. Copy this page's whole address from the address bar.
  2. Open VoiceDebrief, then Settings, then Identity.
  3. Under "A person in the register", paste it where it says "Paste the address you were sent back to", and press "Use the pasted answer".

The app checks that the answer is the one it asked for, that it is for this device's key, and that the signature is the key's it names in the register. If any of that fails it says why and keeps nothing.

What is in the answer

A delegation: a short statement, signed by your key at pki.sgit.ai, saying that the app's key on this device acts for you at this site, for the uses the app asked for, until a date. It holds no secret. Whoever sees it learns which key vouched for which, and nothing that lets them sign as you.

If you arrived here without asking for anything, close the page. Nothing happens unless the address is pasted into the app, and the app refuses an answer it did not ask for.
