# 008: Credit, charging per minute, top-up

**Status:** open, after the account and vault. Needs the owner's price per minute.

On the pattern of newsroom.sgit.ai's SG Meter: £5.00 of starting credit, a few pence per minute of audio
debriefed, the balance allowed below zero, nothing blocked, a charge can be declined with a reason. The
ledger is an append-only log per device in the user's vault. Top-up is an In-App Purchase consumable,
verified on the device with StoreKit.
