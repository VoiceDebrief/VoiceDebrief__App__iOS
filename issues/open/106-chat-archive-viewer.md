# 106: A viewer for chat-archive results, as a separate app

**Status:** open. Recorded at the owner's request, 14 November 2026. Not to be built in the sgit app.

The owner keeps archives of agent sessions in vaults (the web Vault's "Sessions Explorer" shows them: vaults of sessions, with search and statistics). A native viewer for those results should be an app of its own, later, not a screen in the sgit app. Until then they can be shown from the sgit app's gallery, which opens any vault in the web Vault.
