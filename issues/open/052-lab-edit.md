# 052: The lab, seventh experiment (L07): edit with Apple Intelligence

**Status:** open. From the owner's note of 15 November 2026, 11:54, relayed by the parent session.

Start small: pick or paste a paragraph (or a note's transcript), say or type an instruction (make it shorter,
more formal, as bullet points, fix the names), the model proposes the change, shown as a before and after
with the difference marked, accept or reject. Timed and logged like the others.

## Where it stands (15 November 2026)

Built in the Lab. The model is asked for the edited text only; the difference is worked out by the app, so
what is shown is the change itself. Accept replaces the working copy and can be put back. Compiled and unit
tested; no edit has been asked of a model.

## What it does not do yet, and why

- **It does not change a note.** The text is a copy. Writing an accepted edit back into a note's transcript
  or debrief belongs to the session that owns notes, and wants a decision first: an edited transcript is no
  longer what was said.
- **The request is typed**, or dictated with the voice button the design session has put on text fields. It
  has no listening of its own.
- **One text of up to 4,000 characters.** A longer one is cut and the screen says so. Editing a long note in
  parts, as the debrief is made, is the next step if this proves useful.
- **All or nothing.** A proposal is accepted or rejected whole; there is no accepting one change and not another.
- **Names** are helped only by handing the model the names of the contacts.

## What to measure, from the log

Whether the model keeps to the request and leaves the rest alone (the comparison shows it at a glance), how
often it wraps or explains its reply, and the time for a paragraph on each device.
