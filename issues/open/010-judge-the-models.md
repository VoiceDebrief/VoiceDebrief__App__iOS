# 010: Judge Apple's models on real voice notes (spike S2)

**Status:** open, with the owner. This can stop the project.

Build 0.1.1 records or opens a voice note, transcribes it and writes a debrief. On an iPhone, an iPad and a
Mac, with about ten real notes (English and Portuguese, short and long): is the transcript accurate, is the
debrief usable, does European Portuguese work, what does the Diagnostics screen report, how long does each
step take? Share the Diagnostics report after a few runs: it holds timings and sizes, never the words.
