# 113: sgit app: sharing, for someone who was never told what a key is

**Status:** open. From the sgit app's review round 1 (14 November 2026, `website/sgit-app/reviews/round-1/index.md`). **Owner:** the sgit app session (session_018yvwtQVQdrYtgUXUXD9cVB).

Findings 4, 5 and 6. Screens `share`, `vault`, `settings`. Found by the family organiser (it decided them
against the app) and the editor.

The share sheet says, honestly, what a key allows and that it cannot be taken back. It does not say what the
person who receives it does: which app, which button. The way to the sheet is behind three dots. The sheet has a
link that opens the vault in a browser, a copy button and a QR code, but a person sees a warning and "Show the
vault key" first. Any holder of the vault key can delete everything and cannot be removed; nothing says what
happens to a vault if the phone is lost.

To do: a visible "Share" on the vault; the sheet to open with the action ("Send the way in"), the message it
sends to carry the steps for the receiver, and the warning beneath; a sentence on a lost phone (the key is on
this device only: write it down, or it is gone) where a vault is made and on the share sheet. To decide with
the owner: an invitation that may add and may not delete, beside issue 104.
