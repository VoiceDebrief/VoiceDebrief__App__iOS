# 025: The version in plain sight, release notes worth reading, and a briefing on opening

**Status:** open. Asked for by the owner in a voice note, 13 November 2026 (build 0.1.9).

1. The version is easy to find in the app and links to the release notes. Started: a line on the record
   screen (untried on a device).
2. Release notes become short articles: what changed, with pictures of the new screens where possible. The
   pictures can come from the screens work (issue 016).
3. The app notices it has been upgraded and says what is new.
4. On opening, a short briefing written by the on-device model: what is new, what is in the library, whether
   the vault is in step ("I just checked the vault, the vault is fine"). Keep it small.
